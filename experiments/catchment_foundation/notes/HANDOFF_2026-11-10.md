## 1. The goal — read this before optimizing anything

**Long-term objective: an accurate per-gauge representation that makes streamflow forecasts
better**, above all for gauges the forecaster has little or no training data for. The
representation feeds two forecasters:

- the hybrid physics model (`HybridGR4MultiBasin`): a hypernetwork reads GR4/snow parameters
  from the embedding;
- the pure-ML `CrossformerMultiBasin`: the embedding is per-basin context.

Operational forecasting task (from `PROJECT_AUDIT.md`): hourly flow, 14-day horizon, 30-day
spin-up of observed meteorology and flow, state assimilation at issue time; headline metric is
skill vs persistence by lead band (day 1–3, 4–7, 8–14), pooled AND median-basin.

Consequences for how to judge progress:
- **Downstream forecast skill on held-out basins (and held-out years) is the real metric.**
  Signature probes are cheap proxies; in-sample probes are not evidence at all.
- Any claimed gain must beat a **physical-statics-only** context (GAGES-II without the
  streamflow-derived fields) and a **shuffled** context, at matched cohort/windows/seeds.
- For the ungauged case the representation must come from **imagery + statics only**; the
  flow-history tower is unavailable there.

## 7. Results that matter (all nested-α ridge R²; size / flashiness / melt / BFI)

**Representation, held-out basins** (fold 0 = 140 basins never trained on; 1 seed only):

| | size | flash | melt | BFI | fdc slope | high/low |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| embedding (`FLEET_v10_f0_s42`) | 0.44 | **0.25** | 0.43 | **0.36** | **0.36** | **0.62** |
| physical-statics baseline | **0.74** | 0.16 | **0.69** | 0.15 | 0.14 | 0.20 |

The embedding beats statics on every *dynamics* signature on unseen basins and loses on the
*climate-set* ones. In-sample numbers are much higher (melt 0.76, BFI 0.54, flash 0.60) —
much of the earlier signal was transductive. The held-out embedding still uses each basin's
own flow history ("gauged but unseen"), not the ungauged case.

**Representation, in-sample** (for history only): fleet 555 — statics 0.78/0.54/0.79/0.31,
`v8ctl` 0.69/0.59/0.70/0.30. CO/UT 206 — statics 0.60/0.43/0.01/−0.44 (statics collapse
within a region), matched control 0.53/0.47/0.48/0.16, **+log-σ channel 0.56/0.49/0.68/0.40**
(largest single input gain of the project).

**Representation → forecasting** (the only downstream evidence so far; done before this
work, hybrid GR4, CO/UT cohort of 143, 20 `basin_valid` basins, 1 seed, bank v4b — the
pre-fusion-fix, untrained-fusion generation): ordered v4b vs **shuffled** v4b contexts,

38.8 vs 46.0, all-lead 36.7 vs 34.8; median-basin skill negative in every band (−40 to

**Geometry**: a smooth manifold organized by melt/elevation and size gradients, not discrete

## 8. Settled (don't re-run)

Global flow standardization (encodes size); history leakage (pre-2022 cutoff is free);
fleet-scale data alone; fusion just needing training (necessary, not sufficient); fused-view
pairing without modality dropout (shortcut); MLP fusion head (linear wins); wider code (256 =
384 = 512); regional imagery as a 4th tower (hurts); regional imagery as the main vision input
(small BFI gain only); fixed ridge α=10 (hid ~1/3 of signal). GR4 calibrated parameters are not
identifiable (cross-seed ICC 0.45–0.55) — use 3-seed-mean targets
(`calibration/gr4_params_3seed_mean.json`), never single calibrations.

## 9. Known caveats

- **Forecast holdouts and the embedding split are not aligned.** The forecasting manifests
  carry their own `train` / `basin_valid` / `holdout` labels; `basin_split_v1` was built
  independently. Before any forecast comparison, make sure the bank did **not** train on the
  forecast's evaluation basins (needs a small CLI option to hold out an explicit site list, or
  derive forecast holdouts from the split's folds).
- Signatures are proxies: "melt fraction" = Apr–Jul share of flow; BFI = rolling-minimum ratio.
  GAGES-II `BFI_AVE`, `RUNAVE7100`, `WB5100_*` are themselves streamflow-derived (excluded from
  the statics baseline; still inside the model's tabular input).
- 7 of 1,106 regional views have one all-zero band (re-extract when touching the collector).
- `v8ctl` (production) predates the σ channel and the sampler fix.
- The objective is site identity (InfoNCE, ~99% retrieval): similar basins are negatives, so
  nothing explicitly organizes the space by hydrologic behavior.
